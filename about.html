Our Story

We don't just host events; we curate enduring memories steeped in elegance and grandeur.

The Visionaries of Celebration

At Vibe Events & Weddings, we believe that every love story deserves to be told with unapologetic grandeur. Based in the heart of Ernakulam, Kerala, our journey began with a singular vision: to transform ordinary spaces into breathtaking, maximalist dreamscapes.

We blend deep traditional roots with contemporary luxury. Whether it's the rich tapestry of a Hindu wedding mandap or the elegant serenity of a modern reception, our bespoke approach ensures that no two events are ever the same.

Our dedicated team of planners, florists, and designers work tirelessly to curate the perfect vibe. From selecting the finest white orchids and marigolds to orchestrating dynamic Sangeet lighting, we obsess over the details so you don't have to.
